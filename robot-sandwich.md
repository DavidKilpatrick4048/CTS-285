# M1.T – The Sandwich One

## Assumptions

The robot is already powered on, and standing in front of a clean kitchen counter, with hands

The robot can identify the objects named in instructions

Assume there is one dinner plate, two butter knives on the table

Assume there is a table in front of the robot

The loaf of bread is pre-slice and in a plastic bag with twist tie and the bag has at least two intact slices

The jar of peanut butter and jelly are in twist top containers and are both labeled and have peanut butter and jelly in them respectfully.







## Numbered Steps

1\.	Lift the bag directly below the twist tie with left hand

2\.	Grip the twist tie with right hand

3\.	Rotate the bag counterclockwise until it is no longer wrapped around the bread bag

4\.	Pull the twist tie away from the bread bag

5\.	Place the twist tie on table to left side

6\.	Place the bread bag back on table

7\.	Using left hand open the bread bag 

8\.	Use left hand to remove 2 slices of bread from the bag

9\.	Place the two slices of bread of the table, 1 slice next to the other, on their largest sides on the plate

10\.	Grip the loaf bag plastic directly above the remaining bread with left hand

11\.	Squeeze the gripped plastic into a narrow bundle with the left hand

12\.	Pick up the twist tie with right hand

13\.	Wrap the twist tie clockwise around the bundled plastic until the twist tie holds the plastic bag closed.

14\.	Release the twist tie

15\.	Place closed bread bag on table to the left of the plate with two bread slices on it

16\.	Grab the peanut butter container with right hand

17\.	Use left hand to twist the lid of the peanut butter counterclockwise until it comes away from container

18\.	Place the peanut butter lid on counter

19\.	Using empty left hand grip the butter knife by its handle

20\.	Lower the blade of the butter knife into the peanut butter so that only the blade is submerged in the peanut butter

21\.	Move the blade of the butter through the peanut butter until about 1 tablespoon of peanut butter is coating the blade of the butter

22\.	Lift the butter knife out of the peanut butter container

23\.	Touch the peanut butter on the blade to the upward-facing surface of the first bread slice

24\.	Move the blade of the butter knife across the first bread slice until the peanut butter forms an even layer inside the crust edges

25\.	Place the butter knife on the table above the dinner plate.

26\.	Grab the peanut butter container with right hand

27\.	Use left hand to place the peanut butter lid on to of container with its inside surface facing down

28\.	Use left hand to twist peanut butter lid clockwise until it is snug against container

29\.	Use right hand to place peanut butter container on table to the right of the plate with two slices of bread on it

30\.	Use left hand to lift the slice of bread with no peanut butter on it above the plate

31\.	Position the slice of bread with no peanut butter on it over the other slice on the plate, matching its orientation

32\.	Lower the slice of bread on to the peanut butter coated slice, aligning them so they are parallel to each other.

33\.	Release the slice of bread.





## Done When

The two slices of bread are aligned on the dinner plate with the peanut butter between the slices, both peanut butter and the bread loaf bag is closed, and the robots hands are empty.
